# debrief-radio
Traders need news updates the moment they're released to change how they position themselves in the market. Inspired by Bloomberg Radio, this project is a proof-of-concept AI news radio built (with help from Claude Code) that produces customizable feeds for specific traders, fitting their topical needs and update frequencies.

# Inspiration
This summer I spent weeks at a structured credit firm hearing how traders (Commercial Paper) reacted in real time to news and events. Simultaneously, I would listen to Bloomberg Radio for breaking news and industry reactions to get a bit of market context. While I found the information useful as an intern, only around 15% of the radio content would be relevant for many traders, and even less for the Commercial Paper traders I spoke to. Thus, I was inspired to make an MVP of an alternative radio that would use AI to filter relevant news stories for that trader's unique industry or commodity.

# Goal
Develop a proof-of-concept that filters news for specific traders with the help of AI APIs to filter for relevancy.
